# Claude's Underwriting Pattern — 10-Section Handoff Structure

Learned from Claude's v6 analysis of C&J Foodmart + Westbrook Motel (May 2026). This is a superior format for complex going-concern deals where the thesis depends on operational turnaround and unverified seller claims.

## When To Use This Format

Use for deals with:
- Going-concern businesses (c-store, motel, restaurant, gas station)
- Forensic signals (skim, fraud, overstated revenue)
- Dual-offer or multiple structure options
- Environmental liability (USTs, Phase I/II)
- Out-of-footprint deals where buyer can't visit easily
- Seller financing
- Complex partnership/governance questions

## The 10 Sections

### 1. Executive Summary
- One-paragraph deal thesis
- Headline risk/reward (hard floor, worst case, best case MOIC, convexity)
- Verdict FIRST: PURSUE / CONDITIONAL / PASS
- If multiple offers: summarize both with key differentiating factors

### 2. Verified Data (Things We Know)
- Every data point traceable to a primary source
- Source column for each field (Zillow, county, OTA scrape, Z-tape, seller-verbal)
- Split by parcel if multi-parcel deal
- Tax records, FEMA flood zone, parcel IDs
- Operating data if verifiable (Z-tape, POS reports, bank statements)

### 3. Assumptions (Things We Inferred)
- Clearly labeled as ESTIMATES, not facts
- Use low/mid/high triangulation for hard-to-verify items
- Hard asset floor decomposition with specific deductions (UST replacement reserve, FF&E depreciation)
- State-based operating model: Reported → Recovered → Stabilized → Optimized

### 4. Deal Structure Deep-Dive
- Not just price — governance, legal costs, partner risk, exit control
- Dual-offer comparison with cash flow waterfalls
- Seller financing analysis: rate vs TSY spread, balloon structure, personal vs entity debt
- Partner risk: what happens if partner dies/divorces/gets ill?

### 5. Open Verification Items
**Tiered urgency:**
- MUST DO BEFORE LOI (binary gates — if any fail, walk)
- MUST DO BEFORE OFFER (due diligence that affects price)
- MUST DO BEFORE CLOSE (confirmatory, unlikely to kill deal)

### 6. Where The Reviewer Can Add Maximum Value
- Explicit instructions for what to verify independently
- Disproportionate focus on high-conviction but unverified inputs
- Specific actions: call X, scrape Y, verify Z

### 7. Key Contacts & Primary Sources
- Actual phone numbers, not just department names
- Government/regulatory, online sources, industry comps

### 8. Reviewer's Checklist
**Time-tiered:**
- Quick wins (1-2 hours)
- Deeper analysis (1-2 days)
- Skeptic's deep dive (1 week — in-person)

### 9. Documents Produced
- Dashboard version history
- Artifact inventory

### 10. Reviewer Questions To Press The Buyer
- Hard questions the buyer may not have considered
- Personal/family dynamics, liquidity, time commitment, opportunity cost
- "Have you walked away from a similar deal recently? Why?"

## Forensic Signal Patterns

When analyzing going-concern businesses with operator risk:

### C-Store Skim Signals
| Signal | Benchmark | Red Flag |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| Void rate | 1.5-3% | >5% = operator voiding transactions |
| Cash mix | 65-72% | <55% = cash diverted before register |
| Customer count | Positive, steady | Negative = counter manipulation |
| Inside sales/fuel ratio | Industry-dependent | Declining ratio = inside sales pocketed |
| Card sales growth | Should match industry | Card sales rising while cash drops = skim |

### Motel/Lodging Revenue Verification
- Scrape OTA platforms (Booking.com, Expedia) for ADR
- Estimate occupancy from review velocity (review count / period)
- Calculate implied revenue: rooms × ADR × est. occupancy × 365
- Cross-reference against seller's verbal claim

### UST/Environmental Risk Quantification
- Don't model "yes/no" — model probability × replacement cost
- Replacement cost: tanks + piping + dispensing + concrete + engineering
- SC-specific: SCDES case status, SUPERB fund eligibility
- EPA design life: 20-30 years. Tanks at 25+ years = elevated risk

## Seller Financing Analysis

Always compute the spread to risk-free rate:
- Current 10Y TSY rate
- Seller note rate minus TSY = spread
- Typical seller note: TSY + 100-200bps
- Above TSY + 200bps = seller-friendly (use as negotiation leverage)
- Below TSY + 100bps = buyer-friendly (seller wants out)

## "Things I Likely Got Wrong" — Intellectual Honesty Section

Every complex analysis should include this. List specific assumptions that could be wrong with directional impact. Common items:
- Insurance cost (often 50-100% higher than modeled)
- Workers comp (rarely separately modeled)
- Credit card processing (fuel CCs are 3-4%, not 2.5%)
- Tax structure (depreciation, basis, K-1 implications)
- State income tax on distributions
- Opportunity cost of equity
- Natural disaster insurance
- Legal exposure from terminated employees/operators
